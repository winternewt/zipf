## A note on the direction of contamination

Every human tier is dated to before generated text became common. Where any contamination remains it makes a baseline **more** Claude-like, which **shrinks** the measured gap. The bias runs toward the null, so surviving findings are conservative.

The literary tier carries the opposite risk: archaic vocabulary makes ordinary modern words look novel, so it over-reports. That is exactly why a word must clear every tier rather than any one.
